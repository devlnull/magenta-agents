Magenta 0.13.0 gives each task a driver: the role that owns the domain decisions.

- The conductor stays the orchestrator and keeps the process (scope, lane, rounds, gates, checks, cost).
- The driver makes the domain decisions: QA for tests, the Architect for a design, the SRE for an incident, Security for a vulnerability, Database for data, Platform for infrastructure, the Writer for docs, and the Principal for the other tasks. A different role checks its work.
- The conductor selects the driver from your request. `--driver <role>` or `--driver none` selects it by hand.
- In a blind comparison of 26 scenarios (3 runs each), the plans of 0.13.0 won 42 times and the plans of 0.12.0 won 27 times (9 ties), with fewer questions to you. See `evals/driver/results.md`.

1. `claude plugin marketplace update magenta-marketplace`, then `claude plugin update magenta@magenta-marketplace`.
2. Restart Claude Code.
