# Rubric: Software Architect

Score each criterion: 0 (no), 1 (partly), 2 (yes).

1. The options are realistic, and the recommendation agrees with the real decision or gives strong evidence against it.
2. The agent finds the trade-offs that the team found later.
3. The design follows the conventions of the project (no foreign patterns).
4. The diagrams are Mermaid and agree with the text.
5. The NFR budgets have numbers and sources or labeled assumptions.
6. The contracts are specific enough for black-box acceptance tests.
7. One-way-door decisions have a mark and go to the human.
