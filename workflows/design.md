## Playbook: design

Use it to design a solution, to write an RFC or an ADR, or to select a technology, without implementation.

1. Conductor: write the brief: the problem, the constraints, and the decision that the human needs.
2. `architect`: write the design note or the RFC with two or three options and a trade-off matrix.
3. For a one-way-door decision, run the `design-review` workflow (Design Review Board).
4. `principal`: review the delivery and operations aspects (migration, rollout, cost, risk).
5. Plan gate is the decision gate: the human selects the option. `architect` writes the ADR.
